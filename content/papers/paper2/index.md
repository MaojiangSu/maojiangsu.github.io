abstract: We investigate whether a transformer can simulate the training of a deep model through in-context learning. We give an explicit construction of a transformer that carries out gradient descent on a multilayer ReLU network while keeping its own weights fixed. The analysis provides approximation and convergence guarantees and extends to softmax-based transformers. Experiments with three-, four-, and six-layer neural networks on synthetic datasets compare in-context training with direct gradient-based training. This establishes a constructive example of learning a deep model entirely through context.
