abstract: To address the challenge of scarce computational resources in genomic modeling, this paper introduces **GERM**, a genomic foundation model with strong compression performance and fast adaptability. GERM eliminates outliers during both pretraining and fine-tuning stages, enhancing both LoRA-based low-rank adaptation and post-training quantization robustness. Additionally, a continual learning variant named **GERM-T** further reduces retraining costs by applying outlier removal in small steps. Compared to DNABERT-2, GERM improves fine-tuning performance by **37.98%**, quantization robustness by **64.34%**, and reduces average kurtosis by **92.14%**. These results demonstrate GERM's practical effectiveness for genomic tasks in resource-constrained environments.
