abstract: Reinforcement learning is a promising approach for fine-tuning flow matching models. A common strategy uses stochastic SDE inference during training to support exploration, then returns to deterministic ODE inference at deployment. We develop a unified framework based on diffusion-flow duality that covers both reward fine-tuning of generative models and policy optimization with flow policies. We prove that the two inference processes induce close terminal distributions when the flow-matching error is small. We further give sufficient conditions under which policy improvements during stochastic training transfer to deterministic deployment. The analysis explains failure modes caused by weak pretraining or excessive drift during RL. Experiments on synthetic and continuous-control tasks validate the theory and evaluate the resulting algorithms.
