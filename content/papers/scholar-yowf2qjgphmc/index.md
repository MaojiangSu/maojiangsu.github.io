abstract: 'We study in-context universal approximation and compositional generalization in softmax transformers with fixed weights. A prompt specifies the computation to perform, while the transformer serves as an interpreter. We prove that a fixed-weight transformer can approximate a broad class of continuous sequence-to-sequence functions through this in-context emulation mechanism. We also establish composition results: prompts for simpler computations can be combined so that the same transformer executes their composition. The results give a constructive perspective on how a single model can carry out different algorithms and assemble more complex computations without task-specific weight updates.'
