abstract: Diffusion and flow models offer expressive policies for continuous control, but their action likelihoods are generally intractable. This makes many standard policy optimization methods difficult to apply. We introduce Likelihood-Free Generative Policy Optimization (LFGPO), a two-stage framework that separates policy improvement from generative model training. A lightweight ratio model first captures the desired policy update. A conditional drift-matching objective then transfers that update to a diffusion or flow policy. The framework accommodates update rules including PPO and GRPO. Our analysis relates the target policy to the Doob h-transform, proves gradient equivalence between the practical and ideal drift-matching objectives, and establishes a minimum-control-energy interpretation in the diffusion setting. Experiments on MuJoCo continuous-control tasks evaluate both diffusion and flow implementations.
